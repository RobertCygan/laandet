# Safe rental guidelines

> **Informational translation.** The Norwegian version of this document is the legally binding one. If the two versions differ, the Norwegian text prevails.

**Version 0.1.0-utkast · Last updated 25.10.2026**

Good agreements are built on trust, and trust comes more easily when both of you know what has been agreed. Here are
some simple tips that make it safer to rent, borrow and give away.

## Before you agree

- **Read the listing carefully.** Check the condition, known faults, accessories, price, deposit and how the item
  is collected or delivered.
- **Agree on everything in Laandet.** Use the messages in Laandet, so that you both have an overview of what has been
  agreed.
- **Check the profile.** See when the other person became a member, and read any reviews. Remember that a new
  profile without reviews does not mean that anything is wrong.
- **Do not share more than necessary.** Only share your exact address once the agreement has been confirmed, and only
  with the person you have an agreement with.

## Payment – stay alert

- **Laandet never accepts money and never sends payment links.**
- Pay directly to the person you have an agreement with, preferably when you meet. Use cash or Vipps, for
  example.
- Never pay via links you receive in messages. Never give out BankID codes or card details.
- Are you being pressured to pay before you meet, or to pay in an unusual way? Don't do it.
  Report the message.

## At handover

1. **Turn up as agreed.** Agree on a place that suits you both. It can be a public place
   if the item is easy to bring along.
2. **Look at the item together.** Walk around it and look for scratches, dents and other damage.
3. **Test that it works.** Start the engine, switch on the machine or inflate the board. Check that everything works.
4. **Take photos in Laandet.** Take overview photos from several sides, close-ups of any damage and a
   photo of the serial number if there is one.
5. **Go through the accessories.** Check the list in the agreement: charger, keys, straps, vest and so on.
6. **Give a short introduction.** The owner explains how to use the item safely, and what the renter should
   watch out for.
7. **Confirm “Condition at handover”** in Laandet. Do you disagree with the description? Write it down before you take
   the item with you.

## While the item is rented out

- Only use it as you have agreed, and follow the instructions for use.
- Do not lend it on to others unless the owner has said yes.
- Store it safely and in a dry place, and lock it away if needed.
- **If something happens, speak up straight away.** It is much easier to find a solution when you talk
  to each other early.
- If you are going to be late, say so before the deadline runs out, and agree on a new time.

## At return

1. **Clean the item** to a reasonable extent, unless you have agreed otherwise.
2. **Look at the item together**, and test that it works.
3. **Take new photos** from the same angles as at handover.
4. **Check that all the accessories are included.**
5. **Record “Condition at return”** in Laandet. Choose the option that fits:
   - Returned without remarks
   - Returned with normal wear and tear
   - Returned with a recorded deviation
6. **Settle the deposit.** If the owner has no claim, the deposit must be paid back now.

Remember:

> The rented item must be returned in at least as good condition as at handover, except for normal wear and tear resulting from agreed and proper use. (*«Leiegjenstanden skal leveres tilbake i minst like god stand som ved overtakelsen, med unntak av normal slitasje som følge av avtalt og forsvarlig bruk.»*)

## What is normal wear and tear?

| Normal wear and tear (the renter is not responsible) | Not normal wear and tear (the renter may be responsible) |
|---|---|
| A little dirt on wheels and tyres | Dents after the trailer has been overloaded |
| Small marks from use on handles | A cracked cover after the machine has been dropped on the ground |
| A saw blade that is a little duller after ordinary use | A chain that is broken because the saw has been used without oil |
| A battery that has run flat | Accessories that are missing |

Do you disagree about what is normal wear and tear? Read the [Dispute guidelines](dispute-guidelines.md).

## Giving away

- **Describe honestly** what is wrong with the item. Broken or dangerous items must not be described as
  safe and in good order.
- The giver chooses who gets the item. It is fine to say no.
- Confirm the handover in Laandet once the item has been collected.

## Speak up if something is wrong

Do you see a listing, a message or a profile that seems wrong, fake or dangerous? Tap
**“Report”**. It helps us keep Laandet safe for everyone.
