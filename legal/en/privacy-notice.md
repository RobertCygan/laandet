rights you have. We only collect what we need to run the service.

## 2. In short

- We do not ask for your national identity number (fødselsnummer), date of birth or home address.
- Other users only see your display name, the area you live in, when you became a member and actual
  statistics. Your email address is not shown.
- Messages, agreements, condition reports and cases can only be seen by those who are parties to them.
  Laandet staff only see them when you report something, or when you ask for help in a case. Every time a
  staff member sees the content, this is logged.
- We do not use analytics tools, advertising or tracking. We only use cookies that are necessary for
  the service to work.
- We remove location data (GPS) and other metadata from the photos you upload.
- The data is stored in Norway or elsewhere in the EU/EEA.

**Do not share sensitive information.** Do not write about health, political views, religion or similar
in listings, messages or cases. If someone has been injured in connection with a rental, only write what
is necessary for the case.

## 4. Who can see the data

- **Everyone who visits Laandet:** published listings and photos, your display name, your area,
  membership date, actual statistics and published reviews.
- **The other party in a conversation, a rental or a handover:** the messages, the agreement,
  the condition reports, the photos and the agreed meeting place.
- **Laandet's moderators:**
  - messages and content that you or others have reported,
  - the content of a case when one of the parties has asked Laandet for help. Both parties are told about this.

  Moderators do not have access to all private conversations. Every time they see private content, this
  is logged.
- **The police and other public authorities** when the law requires or allows it.

## 6. How long we store the data

| Data | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Account details | As long as you have an account. If you have not logged in for 24 months, we will send you an email. If you do not log in within 60 days after that, we delete the account. |
| Login sessions, with IP address and browser type | Until the session expires, and no longer than 30 days |
| Deleted listings and their photos | 30 days after the listing was deleted |
| Messages | 12 months after the later of these dates: the last message in the conversation, or the end of the last rental in the conversation. Messages used in a case are stored together with the case. |
| Agreements, acceptances, condition reports, photos and history for rentals and handovers | 3 years after the rental or handover has ended. This is the general limitation period for claims. |
| Cases | 3 years after the case is closed. If a party shows that the case is being dealt with by the conciliation board (forliksrådet), in court or by the police, we store the data until those proceedings are finished. |
| Reviews | As long as the account of the person reviewed exists, and no longer than 3 years after the rental |
| Reports, moderation decisions and appeals | 2 years after the case is closed |
| Security log | 24 months |
| Server logs | 30 days |
| Backups | No longer than 6 months |

## 7. When you delete your account

You can delete your account under "My profile". If you have an ongoing rental, a handover or
an open case, it must be closed first. After that, we do the following within 30 days:

- **We delete** your email address, password, introduction, area, listings you saved as favourites, notifications and
  listings that are not linked to a rental, with their photos.
- **We keep** agreements, condition reports, history and cases until the retention period in section 6
  has ended. This data is necessary so that the other party can document any claim. Your display name
  as it appeared in the agreement is part of this documentation. Everywhere else you are shown as
  "Deleted user".
- Messages you have sent can still be seen by the other party, marked "Deleted user", until the
  retention period for messages has ended.
- Backups are deleted automatically within 6 months.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to

- **access** the data we hold about you, and get a copy of it,
- **have incorrect data corrected**,
- **have data deleted**, with the exceptions set out in section 7,
- **restrict** processing, and **object** to processing based on legitimate interest,
- **have your data handed over to you** in a machine-readable format (data portability).

You can download your data under "My profile". You can also write to us at the email address in
section 1. We will reply within one month.

If you believe that we are processing personal data in breach of the rules, you can complain to
**the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet)** at www.datatilsynet.no. We would appreciate it
if you contacted us first.

## 9. Security

We protect your data like this:

- All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Passwords are stored as a cryptographic hash. We cannot see your password.
- No one can see content other than what they are entitled to see. This is checked on the server every time.
- Staff must use two-factor login. Access to private content is logged.
- Backups are encrypted and stored in a different place from the service.
- We remove metadata, such as GPS location, from photos.
- We regularly test restoring from backup.

If a security breach occurs that may affect you, we will notify the Norwegian Data Protection Authority
(Datatilsynet) and you, as required by law.
