# Personal data breach register (GDPR art. 33(5))

Every personal data breach is recorded here — **including the ones that are not notified**.
Procedure: `docs/PRIVACY.md` §9.

1. **Contain** (revoke sessions, rotate secrets, block the vector).
2. **Assess** the risk to users within 24 hours.
3. **Notify Datatilsynet within 72 hours** of becoming aware, if the breach is likely to result in a
   risk to people's rights and freedoms (art. 33). Use Datatilsynet's online breach form
   (www.datatilsynet.no). If some facts are not known yet, notify anyway and add details later.
4. **Inform the affected users** without undue delay if the risk is high (art. 34), in plain Norwegian
   and English.
5. **Record** the breach below, with the reasoning for notifying or not.
