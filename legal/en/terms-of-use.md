## 2. What Laandet is – and what Laandet is not

2.1 Laandet makes it easier to find each other, make arrangements, communicate and document agreements
about lending, renting and handing over things.

2.2 **Agreements to rent, lend or give things are made directly between the users.** Laandet is not a
party to these agreements. Laandet

- does not own the things that are listed,
- is not an insurance company,
- is not a payment institution and does not receive, hold or pay out money,
- does not hold deposits,
- does not guarantee the things, the users or that agreements will be fulfilled,
- does not decide disputes between users with binding effect.

2.3 We will still help when something goes wrong. This is done in line with the [dispute guidelines](dispute-guidelines.md).

## 3. Who can use Laandet

3.1 You must be **at least 18 years old** to create an account. You confirm this yourself when you
register. Laandet does not check your age, and we never state that your age has been checked.

3.2 During the pilot phase, Laandet is **only for private persons**. You cannot use Laandet for business
purposes, for example as a rental company, a shop or a tradesperson who rents out equipment as part of
their work. See section 8.

3.3 Each person may have one account. The account is personal, and you cannot transfer it to anyone
else.

3.4 The information you give us must be correct. Your display name could, for example, be your first
name and the first letter of your surname. It must not give a false impression of who you are.

## 4. Account and security

4.1 You must confirm your email address before you can post listings, send messages or send
requests.

4.2 Keep your password secret, and do not use the same password anywhere else. You are responsible for
what happens with your account, unless someone has gained access without you being able to prevent it.

4.3 Let us know immediately if you think someone else has access to your account. You can log out of
all devices under "My profile".

4.4 Laandet will **never** ask you for your password, BankID codes or payment details by email, in
messages or by phone.

## 5. Listings

5.1 You can post three types of listing:

- **For rent:** You offer a thing for rent against payment, or you lend it out for free.
- **Wanted:** You say what you need.
- **Giving away:** You give a thing away for good, free of charge.

5.2 When you post a listing, you confirm that

- you own the thing or have the right to rent it out, lend it out or give it away,
- the description and photos are correct and show the actual thing,
- you have stated known faults and defects,
- the thing is legal and safe to use in the way the listing describes,
- the declared value is your honest estimate of what it would cost to get an equivalent thing.

5.3 Only give an **approximate location**, for example a village or an area. Do not write your home
address, your phone number or links in the listing. You agree the exact place for collection or for
meeting privately in Laandet once an agreement has been made.

5.4 You can edit, pause, archive and delete your own listings. Changes to a listing do not change an
agreement that has already been made.

5.5 Example listings created by Laandet are clearly marked "Example". They cannot be rented.

## 6. Prohibited and restricted items

6.1 Some things cannot be listed on Laandet. Examples are motor vehicles, motorboats, weapons, fireworks,
drugs, live animals and stolen things. The full list is in [Prohibited and restricted
items](prohibited-and-restricted-items.md).

6.2 Some things require extra care, for example chainsaws, scaffolding, trailers and kayaks. For such
things, the owner and the renter must confirm that they have read the safety information.

6.3 We may remove listings that break the rules. We will then give you a reason, see section 19.

## 7. Ranking of listings

7.1 When you search, we show listings **by time of publication, newest first**. You can choose to sort
by lowest price instead.

7.2 Only listings that match your filters are shown. You can filter by category, type of listing,
area, price, availability and delivery method.

7.3 No one can pay to be placed higher. Laandet has no paid or featured listings.
If this changes, we will clearly mark such listings and update the terms.

## 8. Private persons and businesses (traders)

8.1 All users have themselves confirmed that they use Laandet as private persons. Listings are marked
"Private person" on the basis of this declaration.

8.2 **Agreements between two private persons are not consumer agreements.** Consumer legislation, for
example the rules on the right of withdrawal and on consumer purchases, therefore does not usually apply
between you. The agreement you make, and the general rules of Norwegian law, apply.

8.3 If you act as a business (trader), you must not use Laandet during the pilot phase. We may close
accounts that are used for business purposes.

## 9. Renting and lending

9.1 The renter sends a request through Laandet. **The owner decides whether to accept or decline the
request.**

9.2 When the owner accepts a request, Laandet creates a rental agreement using the details from the
listing and the request:

- Items with a declared value of **5 000 kr or less** get a [simplified rental agreement](simplified-rental-agreement.md).
- Items with a declared value of **over 5 000 kr** must have an [extended rental agreement](extended-rental-agreement.md) with a
  mandatory deposit and stronger identity checks. **Rentals of this kind are not open during the pilot phase.**

The 5 000 kr limit is Laandet's own rule. It does not follow from the law.

9.3 The agreement is made when **both parties have accepted it in Laandet**. Laandet stores the text you
accepted and the time it was accepted.

9.4 The main rule for returning items is:

> **"The rented item must be returned in at least as good condition as at handover, except for
> normal wear and tear resulting from agreed and proper use."**
> (*«Leiegjenstanden skal leveres tilbake i minst like god stand som ved overtakelsen, med unntak av
> normal slitasje som følge av avtalt og forsvarlig bruk.»*)

9.5 Lending for free follows the same rules. The rental price is then 0 kr.

9.6 During the pilot phase, some categories can only be lent for free or given away. This applies to
trailers, bicycles and kayaks, among others. This is stated in the category.

## 10. Giving away

10.1 Things that are given away are free and are given away for good. Laandet does not charge for this.

10.2 The giver chooses who gets the thing.

10.3 The giver must describe the condition and **all known faults**. Broken or dangerous things must not
be described as safe and in working order.

10.4 Once the thing has been handed over, both parties confirm the handover in Laandet with a simple
[transfer confirmation](giveaway-transfer-confirmation.md). No rental agreement is to be made.

## 11. Wanted

11.1 A "Wanted" listing says what you need. Others can reply in messages and point to their own
listings.

11.2 The rental itself always takes place through a "For rent" listing. The usual rules for requests,
agreements and condition reports then apply.

## 12. Payment takes place outside Laandet

12.1 Laandet does not handle money. You pay rent, delivery fees and deposits **directly to each
other**, for example in cash, with Vipps or by bank transfer.

12.2 Payments outside Laandet have **no protection from Laandet**. Laandet cannot reclaim, withhold
or pay out money on your behalf.

12.3 Laandet never sends payment links. Do not pay via links you receive in messages, and never share
BankID codes.

12.4 Laandet is free to use during the pilot phase. Laandet does not take any commission.

## 13. Deposit

13.1 For items with a declared value of 5 000 kr or less, the owner decides whether there should be a deposit.
The amount is shown in the listing and in the agreement, so that the renter knows about it before the
request is sent and before the agreement is accepted. The deposit cannot be higher than the declared value.

13.2 Deposits are paid directly between the parties. **Laandet does not receive or hold deposits.**

13.3 The deposit must be paid back when the item is returned. The owner may withhold part of the
deposit only if the owner has reported a specific claim in Laandet no later than 48 hours after the
return. The owner may then withhold **no more than the amount the claim concerns**. The rest must be paid
back without undue delay.

13.4 A deposit is not insurance. It does not necessarily cover the whole of any loss.

## 14. Cancellation and changes

14.1 Both the owner and the renter can cancel in Laandet before the item has been handed over. Cancel as
early as possible, and feel free to say why. During the pilot phase, no cancellation fee can be agreed in Laandet.

14.2 If you want to change something significant, for example the time, price, deposit, delivery method
or accessories, Laandet creates a new version of the agreement. **Both of you must accept the new
version.** An agreement that has been accepted is never changed without you knowing about it.

## 15. Handover and return

15.1 At handover, you record the condition in "Condition at handover". Taking photos is a good idea.
The renter can check the item and point out any faults before taking it away.

15.2 At return, you record the condition in "Condition at return". Each party writes their own
observations. No one can change what the other party has written.

15.3 If one of the parties does not confirm, Laandet shows that the report is only "confirmed by one
party". Laandet never states that you agree when you have not both confirmed it.

15.4 Please also read the [Safe rental guidelines](safe-rental-guidelines.md).

## 16. Damage and liability between users

16.1 Who is responsible for damage, loss or delay is decided by the agreement between you and by
Norwegian law. It depends on what has been documented, what caused the damage and what the
circumstances were.

16.2 The renter is **not** responsible for damage that existed when the thing was handed over, or for
normal wear and tear.

16.3 The owner must make sure that the thing is in sound condition and matches the description.
The owner must also give the necessary information about use and safety.

16.4 Laandet recommends that you both check what your own insurance covers, for example home contents
insurance or liability insurance. **Laandet has no insurance that covers its users.**

## 18. Reviews

18.1 After a completed rental, the owner and the renter can each give the other one review. The review
covers communication, punctuality, whether the listing was accurate, the condition of the thing and
whether the agreement was followed.

18.2 **Only the parties to a rental that has been registered and completed in Laandet can write a review.**
This is how we make sure that reviews come from people who have actually rented from or to each other.

18.3 Reviews are published at the same time. This happens when both have written their review, or when
the 14-day deadline has passed. That way, no one can see the other party's review before writing their own.

18.4 Reviews must be honest and factual. They must not contain personal attacks, personal data about
others or accusations of criminal offences. You can report a review that you think breaks the
rules. We never change the content of a review. We may remove a review that breaks the rules, and we
will then give a reason.

18.5 Statistics on profiles, for example the number of completed rentals, are calculated only from
actual records in Laandet.
